## Teaching and youth leadership

### Model UN Debate Coach

*Coach · Universidad Pontificia Bolivariana, for public schools in Medellín · Apr – Sep 2025*

Hired by Universidad Pontificia Bolivariana to prepare students from public schools in Medellín for Model United Nations, through classes, simulations and mentoring.

- Classes on fallacies, negotiation, writing proposals, and debate and public speaking.

- [Certificate from the university, in Spanish](https://vvanegasar.github.io/records/model-un-coach-certificate-upb.pdf) (PDF, 770 KB)

### Jóvenes, Ideas que Transforman

*Co-founder and workshop lead · Global Shapers Community (World Economic Forum) · 2025*

A youth project built on workshops in structured problem-solving. Participants map the causes of a problem with a Vester matrix, identify the people who can change it, and turn it into an action plan they present back.
