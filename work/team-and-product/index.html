A team and its products

What it is
Six products for US firms, from a client’s request to production: specialized software, automations and AI agents, built with a team of three developers.
In short

I lead AI and technology for six products built for architecture and engineering, P&C insurance, HR and payroll, PEO, construction and B2B data. A client asks for something; I run the discovery and the demos, turn the request into specialized software, an automation or an AI agent, and take it to production with a team of three developers.

We work AI-first: written goals with a clear “done when”, rules that AI coding agents follow, and a person who decides what ships.

From a client’s request to production

  • Ran discovery with US firms (an architecture and engineering firm, a P&C insurance agency, a structural engineering firm and a specialty subcontractor) through meetings, demos, voice notes and screen recordings, and turned what they asked for into scoped work for the team.
  • Delivered a workload module (timesheets, approvals, budgeted phases and capacity) shaped by a pilot customer’s requirements.
  • Scoped e-signature for insurance forms: mapped each form to its signers, reviewed US e-sign law (ESIGN, UETA) and ran a build-or-buy analysis across six vendors.
  • Led discovery with a specialty subcontractor and reframed the request from “an AI that prices jobs” to “bid on more projects without hiring another estimator”, then built a clickable prototype that treats quantity takeoff as assisted, never autonomous.
  • Ran customer demos myself and built demo environments with fictitious data and bilingual product sheets for five products, so a demo never touches a real customer’s data.

Six products, by industry

  • Lead technology across six products for architecture and engineering, P&C insurance, HR and payroll, PEO, construction and B2B data.
  • My part changes with the product. I am the architect and author of one, I lead product and releases in others, and I audit and set priorities where another developer builds.

AI agents, MCP servers and automations

  • Built and secured MCP servers for three products.
  • Led one of them to production for every customer firm in October 2026, so firms can work from Claude, ChatGPT and Grok. Each firm’s admin turns it on, and the connector is in Claude’s directory. More in AI agents in production.
  • Built automations with n8n: the orchestration of the voice agent, replies to sales inquiries and a daily engineering summary for the team.

Mobile apps

  • Took the mobile apps of Blueprint (architecture and engineering) and Agency Nexus (P&C insurance) to the App Store and Google Play (Nov 2026), a team app and a client app for each: I chose the stack (React Native with Expo), started the iOS base of both and directed the store listings, releases and review submissions.
  • On Blueprint I co-wrote the mobile code with a teammate.
  • Added a room scanner with LiDAR (Apple RoomPlan) to the architecture and engineering app.

The HR and payroll platform

  • Architect and author of the second version of the company’s HR and payroll platform.
  • Rebuilt its prototype as a maintainable codebase and took it to production ten days later.
  • Ran most of its production releases between September and October 2026.

Specialized software and the company’s own code

  • Rebuilt the construction product for contractors on top of the architecture and engineering platform. It is in demo.
  • Led the company’s website and landing pages with the team (sourcyinc.com).
  • Led the company’s move off a no-code AI app builder before its account-deletion deadline, moving code and databases to company-owned infrastructure.

The team

  • Lead a team of three developers, reporting to the CEO.
  • Decide what goes to production. I review the work, define the scope of each version and run the releases.
  • Delegate through written goals with a clear “done when”, instead of day-by-day plans.
  • Wrote the rules AI coding agents follow across the team: what they may do on their own, which checks must pass first, and what always needs a person.
  • Mentor an apprentice developer from SENA, Colombia’s national vocational training service, in working with AI coding agents through written goals and the reasons behind them.
