About

I’m an economist who builds with AI. In 2026 I built the first Power BI dashboard of Antioquia’s Planning Department during my internship, finished my degree and joined a startup as Head of AI & Technology.

I like work I haven’t done before. In my first months there I put an AI voice agent into production, moved the company off a no-code app builder before its deadline, led the launch of an MCP server and took over the technical lead of a product for PEOs. I learn by shipping, and I write down what I learn so the team can use it too.

Before AI, my training ground was the stage: debate, public speaking, Model UN coaching and youth workshops. It still shapes how I lead a team and how I explain technical work to people who don’t write code.
